# TikTok Shop changelog

Substantive updates to this marketplace guide, newest first. Dates are when content was reviewed and published here, not TikTok Shop release dates. Every entry is a documentation review; no app authorization, sandbox, or production call has been tested.

## 2026-11-10

- **Changed:** Expanded the guide from one partner operation into an [API-family directory](apis/index.md) with focused pages for [authorization and tokens](apis/authorization.md), [products](apis/products.md), [orders](apis/orders.md), [fulfillment, logistics, and finance](apis/fulfillment.md), [returns and refunds](apis/returns-refunds.md), and [events](apis/events.md), plus a [development guide](guides/development-guide.md).
- **Reason/source:** TikTok Shop's official [TTS API overview](https://partner.tiktokshop.com/docv2/page/tts-api-concepts-overview) and the Partner Center references linked from each page.
- **Scope:** TikTok Shop TTS API; operation versions, scopes, and market availability remain endpoint-specific.
- **Migration/impact:** None; new pages. Promotion, Seller, Data Reconciliation, and Supply Chain APIs are mapped but have no operation dossiers yet.
